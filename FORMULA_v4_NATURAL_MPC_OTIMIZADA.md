### Fundamento Científico

De acordo com [PMC5657543](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5657543/):
> "Hair Follicle Stem Cells (HFSCs) utilize glycolytic metabolism and produce significantly more lactate than other cells in the epidermis. Lactate generation appears to be critical for the activation of HFSCs."
